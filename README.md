# Smart Career Assistant Using AI

An AI-powered career assistance system developed as part of the IBM AI internship project. The application analyzes a user's resume to extract relevant skills, recommend suitable career roles, evaluate ATS-style skill matching, generate interview questions, provide career improvement suggestions, and create a personalized career roadmap.

## About the Project

Students and job seekers often find it difficult to understand how well their resumes align with industry expectations and which skills they should improve for their target career.

The **Smart Career Assistant** addresses this problem by combining resume analysis, skill extraction, role recommendation, ATS-style matching, and AI-powered career guidance into a single workflow.

The system converts information from a user's resume into practical career-support outputs such as:

- Identified skills
- Recommended career roles
- ATS-style match score
- Missing or relevant skills
- Career improvement suggestions
- Interview preparation questions
- Personalized career roadmap

The project uses **IBM BOB** for AI-assisted career guidance and content generation.

---

## Problem Statement

Students and job seekers may have difficulty determining whether their current skills and resume align with the requirements of their desired career roles.

Common challenges include:

- Limited awareness of Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS)
- Difficulty identifying skill gaps
- Lack of personalized career guidance
- Insufficient interview preparation
- No clear learning or career roadmap

The project aims to provide an accessible AI-assisted solution that helps users better understand their current profile and identify practical areas for career development.

---

## Objective

The main objective of the project is to develop an AI-powered career assistant that can:

1. Analyze uploaded resumes.
2. Extract relevant technical and professional skills.
3. Recommend suitable career roles.
4. Perform ATS-style skill matching.
5. Identify missing or relevant skills.
6. Generate personalized career suggestions.
7. Generate interview questions.
8. Provide a structured career roadmap.

---

## Key Features

### 1. Resume Upload & Parsing

Users can upload their resume for automated text extraction and analysis.

### 2. Skill Extraction

The system identifies relevant skills from the resume and presents them as a structured skill profile.

### 3. Role Recommendation

Based on the extracted skills, the system recommends potentially suitable career roles.

### 4. ATS Match Analysis

The application compares candidate skills with required skills and generates an ATS-style compatibility score.

### 5. Skill Gap Identification

The system highlights skills that may be missing from the candidate's current profile.

### 6. AI Career Suggestions

IBM BOB is used to generate personalized suggestions for improving the candidate's career profile.

### 7. AI Interview Preparation

The system generates interview questions relevant to the candidate's skills and career profile.

### 8. Personalized Career Roadmap

The application generates a structured roadmap to help users plan their future learning and career development.
